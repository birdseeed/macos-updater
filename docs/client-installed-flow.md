## Explicit dispatch, failure, recovery and UI readback

Only explicit owner action may dispatch. Selection/prepare does not execute;
background requests refuse. One child/readback lease spans the entire action,
including cancellation or a changed selection; no second child may overlap.
Each selection change or cancel invalidates earlier results, without claiming
to stop an already executing transaction. Revalidate held candidate/target/
binary/receipt/markers immediately before dispatch. Refusal leaves evidence and
does not silently retry, recover, sign, launch, resume, signal or clean up.

The live gateway bounds retained output, drains to natural exit and observes actual child exit,
exact stdout and newly written durable report. Bind action/run/root/target,

mount absence. Only then may update display success. Recovery and restore may
display restored only after fresh verified157.0 target/baseline equality.
