## Internal privacy and retention requirements

PAT-2605 retention and trial-marker durations below are owner-agreed requirements, not deployed behavior. Exact cleanup, legal basis and operational proof remain open.

- Keep app inventory and catalogue matching on-device. Do not encode inventory in URLs, logs, analytics or licence requests. A catalogue download and local-only matching still produce ordinary network metadata; do not promise otherwise.
- Under the 12 October override, bounded automatic failed-update reports default ON with visible opt-out ([PAT-2665](automatic-failure-report-integration-brief.md)). Manual support includes diagnostics by default with exact preview, opt-out and explicit user submission ([PAT-2664](manual-support-form-integration-brief.md)); opening Help or preview never sends. General crash/error reports and feature analytics still require independent opt-ins; coverage sharing also remains opt-in. Current ordinary composition sends no reports or support messages. Withdrawal blocks unsent-buffer sends under the applicable category policy. No reporting/analytics provider or destination is selected. Limit evidence to the selected app and purpose, redact secrets and personal paths, name destination and retention, and allow refusal. No app list or diagnostics were collected or sent for this matrix.
- Keep account/device activation records separate and minimal. Agreed routine retention is 24-hour challenges/counters, 30-day expired/revoked sessions and ordinary API retries, 90-day deactivated Mac records, and 24-month minimal audit; active account records persist while the account exists. The retention policy is agreed; exact cleanup behavior, legal basis and operational verification remain unproved. Billing/financial records follow separate provider/legal retention.
- On account deletion, retain a protected trial-used marker for exactly 12 months from the original deletion. Re-registration does not extend it; after the 12 months, a new account is eligible for a trial. This policy is agreed; its legal basis, disclosure and privacy review remain gates before launch. Define and verify cleanup without extending the original deadline. Optional notes/vendor URL and verified household request authentication are settled. Exact diagnostics/request retention, private-access implementation and privacy review remain technical launch gates. Do not silently collect attachments, broad logs, hardware fingerprints or cross-user inventory.
- Catalogue evidence and service operations need their own retention/access rules; raw customer inventory is not a catalogue input. Unknown or inaccessible app state must remain honestly unknown.
